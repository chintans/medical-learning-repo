# Glossary

| Term | Plain meaning | Roots | Where you will see it |
|---|---|---|---|
| hepat- | liver | Greek *hēpar* | hepatitis, hepatomegaly on USG reports; LFT = liver function test |
| nephr- / ren- | kidney | Greek *nephros* / Latin *renes* | nephrology, nephropathy; "renal function test" (KFT/RFT) |
| cardi- | heart | Greek *kardia* | cardiology, tachycardia |
| -itis | inflammation | Greek *-itis* | hepatitis, appendicitis, arthritis |
| -megaly | enlargement | Greek *megas* (large) | hepatomegaly, splenomegaly, cardiomegaly (USG / X-ray reports) |
| -pathy | disease of | Greek *pathos* (suffering) | nephropathy, neuropathy (diabetes complications) |
| -aemia / -emia | condition of the blood | Greek *haima* (blood) | anaemia, hyperglycaemia, hyperbilirubinaemia |
| -penia | deficiency, too few | Greek *penia* (poverty) | thrombocytopenia (dengue!), leucopenia on CBC |
| hyper- / hypo- | above / below normal | Greek | hypertension, hypothyroidism, hypoglycaemia |
| tachy- / brady- | fast / slow | Greek *tachys* / *bradys* | tachycardia, bradycardia (ECG reports, vitals) |
| chol(e)- | bile | Greek *cholē* | cholecystectomy, cholesterol |
| cyst- | bladder or sac | Greek *kystis* | cholecystitis (gallbladder), cystitis (urinary bladder) |
| lapar- | abdomen / abdominal wall | Greek *lapara* (flank) | laparoscopy, laparotomy |
| -ectomy | surgical removal | Greek *ek-* (out) + *tomē* (cut) | appendectomy, nephrectomy |
| -otomy | surgical incision (cut into) | Greek *tomē* (cut) | laparotomy, tracheotomy |
| -ostomy | surgical opening to the surface (stoma) | Greek *stoma* (mouth) | colostomy, tracheostomy |
| -scopy | viewing with an instrument | Greek *skopein* (to look) | endoscopy, laparoscopy, colonoscopy |
| -logy | study of | Greek *logos* | gastroenterology, cardiology |
| peri- | around | Greek | pericarditis, perianal |
| k/c/o, c/o, h/o, s/p | known case of / complains of / history of / status post | English and Latin clinical shorthand | Indian case sheets and discharge summaries |
| OD, BD, TDS, HS, SOS | once, twice, three times daily; at bedtime; only when needed | Latin *omni die*, *bis die*, *ter die sumendum*, *hora somni*, *si opus sit* | Indian prescriptions |
| gastr- | stomach | Greek *gastēr* | gastritis, gastrectomy, gastroenterologist |
| pneum(on)- / pulmon- | lung (Greek / Latin); pneum- also means air | Greek *pneumōn* (lung), *pneuma* (air); Latin *pulmo* | pneumonia, pulmonary embolism, pneumothorax on chest X-ray reports |
| -thorax | chest | Greek *thōrax* | pneumothorax (air in the chest cavity), haemothorax |
| neur- | nerve | Greek *neuron* | neuropathy, neuralgia, neurologist |
| haem(at)- / hem- | blood | Greek *haima* | haematology, haemorrhage, haematoma |
| oste- | bone | Greek *osteon* | osteoporosis, osteomyelitis; orthopaedics for bone/joint specialists |
| derm(at)- | skin | Greek *derma* | dermatitis, dermatologist |
| cutane- | skin (Latin, anatomical adjective) | Latin *cutis* | cutaneous rash; subcutaneous injection (insulin) |
| -oma | mass or swelling; often a tumour, not always, and never by itself "cancer" | Greek *-ōma* | lipoma (benign), carcinoma (malignant), haematoma (collected blood) |
| -algia | pain (a symptom, not a cause) | Greek *algos* | arthralgia, myalgia (fever cases in monsoon), neuralgia |
| -osis | abnormal condition or process | Greek *-ōsis* | osteoporosis, fibrosis, tuberculosis |
| lip(o)- | fat | Greek *lipos* | lipoma, lipid profile |
| carcin- | cancer (of epithelial tissue) | Greek *karkinos* (crab) | carcinoma, carcinogen |
| mast- / mamm- | breast (Greek / Latin) | Greek *mastos*; Latin *mamma* | mastectomy, mammography |
| hyster- | uterus | Greek *hystera* | hysterectomy; "uterine" is the Latin adjective |
| stomat- | mouth | Greek *stoma* | stomatitis (mouth ulcers); "oral" is the Latin adjective |
| arthr- / my- | joint / muscle | Greek *arthron* / *mys* | arthritis, arthralgia, myalgia |
