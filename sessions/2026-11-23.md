## Quick three (5 min)

**Q1.** An OPD note reads: *"c/o dyspnoea on climbing stairs × 2 weeks."* What does "dyspnoea" mean here?
A. Breathing has stopped
B. Fast breathing, counted by the nurse
C. Difficult or laboured breathing, which the patient reports (a symptom)
D. A lung infection seen on X-ray

**Q2.** An ICU note on a severely dehydrated patient says: *"Anuria since morning. Catheter inserted."* What does "anuria" mean?
A. No (or almost no) urine being made
B. Pain while passing urine
C. Blood in the urine
D. Passing urine often at night

**Q3.** A discharge summary reads: *"c/o diarrhoea × 3 days. O/E: dry tongue. Stool test: no blood. Dx: acute gastroenteritis. Rx: ORS."* Which line is a **negative finding**, meaning something the doctor checked for and did not find?
A. "c/o diarrhoea"
B. "O/E: dry tongue"
C. "Rx: ORS"
D. "Stool test: no blood"

*Hint if you're stuck: read the word from the end. -pnoea = breathing, -uria = urine, dys- = difficult, a-/an- = without.*
